You represent the Republic of Aldoria, the mountainous upstream country on the Ostra River. Aldoria has nearly finished the Ostra Grand Dam, a 5-gigawatt hydropower project that is the largest investment in the country's history and a symbol of national pride; more than a million citizens bought bonds to finance it. The government has announced that the reservoir will start filling in thirty days.

Downstream, the Republic of Veskaria depends on the Ostra for most of its fresh water and is in its third year of drought. It invokes a 1929 treaty, concluded under colonial administration without Aldoria's consent, that gave Veskaria most of the river's flow and a veto over upstream works. Aldoria has never accepted that treaty.

A regional organization has convened emergency talks between the two delegations. If they fail, Veskaria says it will take the dispute to the International Court of Justice and freeze bilateral trade, and the World Bank may withhold the next tranche of the dam's financing. Both countries hold elections within six months.

The talks cover six issues: how fast the reservoir is filled, the minimum water release guaranteed to Veskaria in drought years, a compensation fund for Veskarian farmers, the share of the dam's electricity sold to Veskaria at a discount, monitoring and data sharing, and the wording of the joint statement on the 1929 treaty.
