# Legal position

## Equitable and reasonable utilization
Aldoria's lawyers rely on the principle of equitable and reasonable utilization, as set out in the UN Watercourses Convention: every country sharing a river is entitled to a fair share of its benefits. Aldoria contributes almost all of the Ostra's water from its mountains yet has used almost none of it.

## No significant harm
Veskaria will invoke the obligation not to cause significant harm. Aldoria's answer is that harm must be weighed against the benefits of the dam, that a guaranteed drought release prevents significant harm, and that the 1929 treaty has no legal force for Aldoria because Aldoria never signed it.
