# Earlier negotiations

## 2019 and 2023
In 2019 talks collapsed when Aldoria refused to discuss the 1929 treaty. In 2023 a framework on data sharing was nearly agreed, but Aldoria insisted on filling the reservoir in three years regardless of drought, and the Veskarian delegation walked out.

## Lessons
Aldorian negotiators react strongly to any challenge to their sovereignty over the dam, but they have shown flexibility on technical arrangements and on energy trade.
