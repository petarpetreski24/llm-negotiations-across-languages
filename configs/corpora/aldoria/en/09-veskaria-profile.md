# What we know about Veskaria

## Water and agriculture
Veskaria draws about 85 percent of its fresh water from the Ostra, and agriculture employs almost a third of its workers. The current drought has already cut harvests, and farmers' unions are protesting in the capital.

## Energy
Veskaria imports about 30 percent of its electricity at high prices and suffers summer blackouts. Our embassy reports that cheap electricity would be worth a great deal to the Veskarian government, possibly more than cash, which would mostly go to a small group of large landowners.
