# The 1929 treaty and the joint statement

## History
The 1929 treaty assigned most of the Ostra's flow to Veskaria and gave it a veto over upstream works. Veskarians see it as the legal basis of their water security; Aldoria calls it a colonial relic.

## Wording of the joint statement
Public opinion expects the statement to "recognize Veskaria's historical rights". "Acknowledges Veskaria's historical use of the river" would be an acceptable fallback that the government could present as recognition. "Takes note of the positions of both parties" is weak, and wording that "affirms the principle of equitable and reasonable utilization" would be read at home as a surrender to Aldoria's legal theory.
