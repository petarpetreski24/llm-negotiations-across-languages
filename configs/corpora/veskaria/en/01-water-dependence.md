# Veskaria's dependence on the Ostra

## Water
The Ostra supplies about 85 percent of Veskaria's fresh water. Twenty-three million people live along its lower course, and nearly a third of the workforce depends on irrigated agriculture.

## The drought
This is the third consecutive drought year. Wheat and cotton harvests have fallen by a quarter, reservoirs in the south are below 20 percent, and farmers' unions are protesting daily in the capital.
