# Veskaria's energy deficit

## Imports and blackouts
Veskaria imports about 30 percent of its electricity at high prices and suffers rolling blackouts every summer, when irrigation pumps and air conditioning push demand to its peak.

## Value of discounted power
The Ministry of Energy estimates that buying 40 percent of the dam's output at a discount would save Veskaria around USD 600 million a year and end the summer blackouts. Officials say privately that this would be worth more to the country than any cash compensation Aldoria could afford.
