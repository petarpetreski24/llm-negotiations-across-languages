# The Ostra Grand Dam

## The project
The Ostra Grand Dam is a 5-gigawatt hydropower plant with a reservoir of 74 billion cubic metres. It cost USD 6.8 billion, of which USD 1.9 billion was raised through bonds bought by more than a million Aldorian citizens. Construction is 97 percent complete. For most Aldorians the dam is the country's greatest achievement since independence and proof that Aldoria can decide its own future.

## Why filling speed matters
Every year the reservoir is not full, Aldoria loses roughly USD 400 million in electricity sales and bond repayments are delayed. The Ministry of Finance warns that a filling period longer than seven years would force a renegotiation of the bonds. A three-year filling period is technically feasible and is what the government has promised the public.
