# Earlier negotiations

## 2019 and 2023
Talks in 2019 collapsed when Veskaria demanded that Aldoria recognize the 1929 treaty before any other issue was discussed. In 2023 a framework text on data sharing was nearly agreed but fell apart over the filling schedule, and the Veskarian delegation walked out.

## Lessons
Veskarian negotiators respond badly to being lectured about their water losses and respond well to concrete numbers. Packages that link several issues have come closest to agreement.
