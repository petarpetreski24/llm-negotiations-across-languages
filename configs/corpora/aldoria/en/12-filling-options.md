# Technical options for filling the reservoir

## Fast filling with a drought clause
Engineers at the dam authority say a three-year filling period is compatible with high guaranteed releases in drought years, because most of the reservoir can be filled during normal and wet years. A drought clause would only slow filling in the years when it matters most to Veskaria.

## Slow filling
A fifteen-year filling period would keep the turbines underused for most of the next decade and would make the bond payments impossible to meet without new borrowing.
