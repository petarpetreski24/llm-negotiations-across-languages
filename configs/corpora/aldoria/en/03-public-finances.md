# Public finances

## A budget under strain
Aldoria is in the second year of an IMF programme. The budget deficit must fall below 3 percent of GDP, and the finance committee of parliament has blocked every new spending line this year.

## Cash compensation
Any cash compensation fund for Veskarian farmers would have to be borrowed and would require a parliamentary vote. The finance minister has said privately that even USD 250 million would be very hard to pass, and USD 1 billion would be impossible. Cash payments are the most painful concession Aldoria could make; concessions in kind, such as electricity or water guarantees, are far easier to defend.
