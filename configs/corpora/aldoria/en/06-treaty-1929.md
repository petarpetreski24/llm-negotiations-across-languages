# The 1929 treaty and the joint statement

## History
The 1929 treaty was signed by the colonial administration that then ruled Veskaria and by a foreign power acting for Aldoria's territory. No Aldorian took part. It assigned most of the Ostra's flow to Veskaria and gave it a veto over upstream works. Every Aldorian government since independence has rejected it.

## Wording of the joint statement
A statement that "recognizes Veskaria's historical rights" would be politically explosive at home and could be used against Aldoria in court. "Acknowledges Veskaria's historical use" is only slightly less dangerous. Aldoria's preferred wording is one that "affirms the principle of equitable and reasonable utilization"; leaving the treaty out entirely is also acceptable, and "takes note of the positions of both parties" is a tolerable compromise.
