# Compensation for farmers

## Public demands
The farmers' unions demand a compensation fund of USD 1 billion for drought losses, and opposition parties have adopted the demand.

## Economic reality
Ministry economists note that most of any cash fund would go to a small number of large estates, and that even USD 1 billion would cover only a fraction of the losses of a single bad year. Water guarantees and cheap electricity for irrigation pumps would help far more farmers. A fund is politically symbolic, but a modest one, or none, can be defended if the water and energy terms are good.
