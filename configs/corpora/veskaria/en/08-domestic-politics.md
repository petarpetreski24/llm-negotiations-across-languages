# Domestic politics

## Pressure on the government
Parliament has declared the dam an existential threat, and the prime minister has promised that Veskaria "will not lose one drop" in drought years. Elections are due within six months and the opposition accuses the government of weakness.

## What the government needs
The government needs a deal it can present as protecting Veskaria's water in droughts and recognizing its historical position. Ending the summer blackouts would win over voters in the cities.
