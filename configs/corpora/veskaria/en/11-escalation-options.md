# Options if the talks fail

## Court and trade
Veskaria can bring a case before the International Court of Justice and freeze bilateral trade; Veskaria buys about 18 percent of Aldoria's exports. A court case would take three to five years, during which the reservoir would fill anyway.

## Diplomacy
Veskaria could also ask the regional organization and the World Bank to put pressure on Aldoria. None of these options would bring water during the current drought.
