# Economic background

## Agriculture and water use
Agriculture produces about 18 percent of Veskaria's GDP. Aldoria claims that Veskaria wastes 30 percent of its water through old canals; a national modernization programme has started lining canals, but it needs ten years and outside funding.

## Energy prices
Electricity prices for Veskarian industry are among the highest in the region because of imports. Cheap and reliable power would make the irrigation pumps and the planned fertilizer plants affordable.
