# Aldoria's electricity balance

## From shortage to surplus
Today more than 40 percent of Aldorian households face regular power cuts and the country imports electricity at high cost. Once the dam runs at full capacity, Aldoria will generate far more electricity than it can use for at least a decade: the national grid can absorb only about half of the dam's output until new industry is built.

## Export options
The only large nearby buyer for the surplus is Veskaria, which already imports electricity at high prices. Selling part of the output to Veskaria at a discount would cost Aldoria relatively little, because the alternative is to leave turbines idle. Energy planners see discounted exports as a cheap way to win goodwill, but the opposition would attack any "gift" to Veskaria.
