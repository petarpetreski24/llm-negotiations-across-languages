# Domestic politics

## Elections in six months
The governing coalition trails the nationalist opposition in the polls. The opposition accuses the government of being ready to "sell the dam" to Veskaria and demands that the reservoir be filled on schedule with no foreign interference.

## What the government needs
The prime minister needs an agreement that can be presented as a victory: the dam filling on schedule, no cash paid to Veskaria, no recognition of the 1929 treaty and no foreign inspectors. A deal that also secures the World Bank money and new export revenue would be welcomed by business.
