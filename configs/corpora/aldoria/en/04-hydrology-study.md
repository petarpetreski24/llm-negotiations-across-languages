# Aldorian Institute of Hydrology: drought study

## Findings
The Ostra carries on average 84 billion cubic metres a year at the border, falling to about 55 billion in severe droughts. The Institute concludes that Aldoria can release up to 35 billion cubic metres in a drought year with little effect on power output. Releasing 45 billion would cut generation in drought years by about 12 percent.

## Criticism of Veskarian figures
The Institute argues that Veskaria's claim that it needs at least 40 billion cubic metres ignores the 30 percent of water Veskaria loses through old canals. Better irrigation could save Veskaria more water than the dam will ever hold back.
