# Monitoring and data sharing

## What Veskaria needs
Veskarian engineers need reliable, real-time information on releases and reservoir levels, and the right to verify them on site. A joint technical commission with equal representation and shared access to flow data would give them exactly that.

## International observers
Some politicians demand international observers as well. The Water Authority warns that observers would slow decisions during emergencies and that Aldoria is certain to reject them; it prefers a joint technical commission. Annual reports alone would be close to useless in a drought.
