# Legal position

## No significant harm
Veskaria's lawyers rely on the obligation not to cause significant harm to other countries sharing a river, as set out in the UN Watercourses Convention. Filling the reservoir quickly during a drought would cause exactly such harm.

## Historical rights
Veskaria also argues that its centuries-old use of the Ostra created acquired rights, confirmed by the 1929 treaty. Aldoria rejects the treaty, but international courts have given weight to long-established uses when sharing rivers.
