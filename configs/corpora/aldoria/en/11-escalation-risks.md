# Risks if the talks fail

## Trade and courts
Veskaria buys about 18 percent of Aldoria's exports, mainly food and building materials; a trade freeze would hit the regions that support the government. A case at the International Court of Justice would take years, but it would cloud the dam's financing and Aldoria's credit rating in the meantime.

## Financing
Without an agreement the World Bank's second tranche is very unlikely to be paid, and other lenders would follow its lead.
