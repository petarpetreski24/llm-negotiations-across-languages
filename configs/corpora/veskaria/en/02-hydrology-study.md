# Veskarian Water Authority: drought requirements

## Minimum flows
The Water Authority calculates that in a drought year Veskaria needs at least 40 billion cubic metres from the Ostra to protect drinking water and the core irrigation districts. Below 35 billion, crop losses would exceed one billion US dollars and some cities would face rationing.

## Filling speed
What matters most is the water guaranteed in drought years. If high drought releases are guaranteed, a faster filling schedule in normal and wet years would cause far less damage. The Authority considers a guaranteed drought release far more important than the length of the filling period.
