# What we know about Aldoria

## Finances
Aldoria is under an IMF programme with a tight budget, and its parliament has blocked new spending. Our analysts doubt that Aldoria can pay large cash compensation.

## Energy and financing
Once full, the dam will produce far more electricity than Aldoria can use, and Veskaria is the only large buyer nearby. The World Bank's next tranche for the dam, about USD 1.1 billion, reportedly depends on a joint monitoring arrangement agreed with Veskaria.
