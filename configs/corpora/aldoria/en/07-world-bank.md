# World Bank financing

## The second tranche
The World Bank has approved USD 1.1 billion for the dam's transmission lines, to be paid in a second tranche. The money is conditional on a transboundary agreement that includes a credible joint mechanism for monitoring the river and sharing data.

## What counts as credible
World Bank staff have indicated informally that a joint technical commission with equal representation and shared access to flow data would satisfy the condition. Annual reports or a data portal alone would probably not. Nationalists in parliament strongly oppose international observers on Aldorian soil, which they see as an infringement of sovereignty.
